        Practice use only

        BladeBot is a training tool. Only use it where everyone involved is fine with it:

        • the built-in practice arena (offline simulator) - always safe,
        • Blade Ball's Training Mode (Server Select, blue button at the bottom right), ideally with Observe only switched on, or
        • private servers with friends who know you are testing a bot.

        Know the risk: Blade Ball's rules ban auto-parry programs, and that includes external programs like BladeBot that press the block key for you. Letting it press keys in Blade Ball, even in Training Mode, can get your account banned from the game. Observe only never presses anything: it shows when the network would parry, so you can compare its timing with your own.

        Never use it in public matches or ranked games. It spoils the game for other players and can get your account banned. You are responsible for how you use it. BladeBot is not affiliated with Roblox or the Blade Ball developers.

        How it works

        1. Capture - grabs the middle of your screen with mss.
        2. Vision - finds "targeting red" pixels, groups them into blobs and picks the round ball. It also checks whether your own character is highlighted red (the game does this when the ball targets you).
        3. Tracking - fits the ball's position and size over the last few frames: how fast it grows on screen (looming), how fast it moves towards your character, how far away it is...
        4. Neural network - a small multilayer perceptron (NumPy only) turns those 19 features into the probability that the ball hits you within 0.05, 0.10, ... 1.0 seconds. It was trained on tens of thousands of simulated approaches where the true impact time is known.
        5. Decision - when the chance of impact within your lead time passes the threshold, BladeBot presses the block key once for that ball.
